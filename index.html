    How to play

    Tap or click a square to sweep it. Follow stronger readings across nearby squares, then dig where the meter peaks. A full meter means metal is directly below, but it could be treasure or scrap.

    Your sweep budget drops by five each location. Sweeping a new tile uses one sweep. You can recheck scanned tiles without spending another. Junk and ghost targets (empty holes) use a dig and are marked with ×. Good finds, including modern clad coins, are marked with ★. If you run out of sweeps, you can still dig any peaks you already located. Find the required treasures to advance. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move between tiles, Enter or Space to sweep, and D to dig.
